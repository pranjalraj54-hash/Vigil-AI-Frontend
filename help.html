How Vigil-AI works

What is Vigil-AI?

Vigil-AI is a video intelligence platform that analyzes CCTV footage, identifies meaningful events, and helps security teams investigate incidents without manually reviewing hours of video. It's an intelligence layer for the cameras you already have — not another dashboard to replace them.

How it works

01

Upload / Connect

Upload recorded footage or connect live camera feeds.

02

Detect

Vigil-AI identifies people, vehicles, objects, zones, and unusual activity.

03

Understand

Events are organized into timelines and incidents with contextual explanations.

04

Investigate & Report

Review relevant footage and generate an evidence-based report.

What can Vigil-AI detect?

People Vehicles Restricted-area entry Loitering Unattended objects Possible weapons Crowd activity Other configurable events
Prototype examples — a production deployment would be configured per site.

How to use Vigil-AI

On the Home page

  1. Upload footage
  2. Review the AI-generated timeline and report
  3. Click a timestamp to jump to that moment

On Live Monitoring

  1. Open Live Monitoring
  2. Watch active camera feeds
  3. Review AI alerts as incidents are detected

Understanding AI results

HIGH CONFIDENCE

The system has strong visual evidence for the detected event.

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

The event may require human verification.

POSSIBLE EVENT

The system detected visual patterns associated with the event but this should not be treated as definitive.
